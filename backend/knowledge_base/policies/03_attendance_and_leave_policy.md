# Attendance and Leave Policy

Company: Orion Nexus Technologies Pvt. Ltd. (fictional) | Policy owner: Human Resources | Version 3.0

## 1. Shifts
- **General shift:** 09:30 to 18:30, Monday to Friday.
- **Morning shift:** 07:00 to 16:00 (support and operations teams).
- **Evening shift:** 14:00 to 23:00 (support and operations teams).
Each employee's shift is shown on their profile. Changing shift needs the reporting manager's approval in the HR portal.

## 2. Check-in and late marks
Employees check in at the entrance using face verification or their ID card. A grace period of 15 minutes after the shift start is allowed. Checking in more than 15 minutes late is a late mark. Three late marks in a calendar month count as half a day of casual leave.

## 3. Working hours
The expected working time is 9 hours a day including a 1-hour lunch break. Working fewer than 4.5 hours in a day counts as half a day of leave unless approved by the manager.

## 4. Leave entitlement (per calendar year)
| Leave type | Days | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Casual leave | 12 | Apply at least 1 day in advance; at most 3 days in a row |
| Sick leave | 10 | A doctor's certificate is needed for more than 2 days in a row |
| Earned leave | 18 | Apply 15 days in advance; up to 30 days can be carried forward |
| Maternity leave | 26 weeks | As per applicable law |
| Paternity leave | 10 days | Within 3 months of the child's birth |
Interns get 1 casual leave per month of internship and no earned leave.

## 5. Work from home
Employees may work from home up to 2 days a week with their manager's approval. WFH is not allowed during the first 3 months after joining, and not for roles that need physical access to the R&D lab or server room on that day.

## 6. Being on leave
An employee whose status is On Leave should not come to the office. If they need to visit (for example to collect equipment), they need written approval from HR or their manager, and they cannot enter L3 or L4 zones during the leave.

## 7. Public holidays
The company follows 12 public holidays a year, published by HR every December. Office access on a public holiday needs manager approval.
