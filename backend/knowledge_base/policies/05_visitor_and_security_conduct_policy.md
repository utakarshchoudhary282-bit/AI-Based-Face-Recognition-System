# Visitor Management and Security Conduct Policy

Company: Orion Nexus Technologies Pvt. Ltd. (fictional) | Policy owner: Corporate Security | Version 2.0

## 1. ID cards
Employees must wear their ID card visibly at all times inside the office. A lost ID card must be reported to Corporate Security within 2 hours. A temporary card is issued after face verification or a manual check, and is valid for 3 days. Lending an ID card to anyone is a security violation.

## 2. Visitors
- Visitors must be pre-registered by their host employee at least 4 hours before arrival.
- At reception, the visitor shows a government photo ID and receives a visitor badge.
- Visitors stay in the lobby and ground-floor meeting rooms unless escorted by their host.
- Visitors may never enter L4 zones (server room, finance vault).
- Visitor badges are returned at exit; the visit is closed in the visitor register.

## 3. Former employees
A person whose status is Exited is treated as a visitor. They need a host employee and a visitor badge, and they may not enter any zone above L1.

## 4. Contractors and vendors
Contractors get an L1 access level by default. Vendor engineers working in the server room need a valid work order, must be escorted by an L4 employee the whole time, and must sign the vendor register.

## 5. Tailgating
Do not hold secured doors open for others and do not follow someone through a secured door. Each person must verify individually. Security staff who see tailgating must stop the person and run a verification.

## 6. Reporting incidents
Report security incidents (lost cards, tailgating, unknown persons, failed verifications that look suspicious) to Corporate Security at extension 4444 or security@orionnexus.example. Emergencies: call the Security Control Room at extension 100.

## 7. Escalation contacts
- Security Supervisor (on duty, 24x7): extension 4400
- Head of Security: approves L4 access and investigates violations
- HR Business Partner: employee status, leave approvals and suspensions
