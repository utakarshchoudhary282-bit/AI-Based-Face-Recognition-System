# Access Control Policy

Company: Orion Nexus Technologies Pvt. Ltd. (fictional company used for this demo)
Policy owner: Corporate Security | Version 2.1 | Applies to: all employees, contractors and interns

## 1. Purpose
This policy defines which areas of the office each employee may enter, based on their access level. Every employee is assigned exactly one access level (L1 to L4) by HR at joining. The access level is shown on the employee's verification card in the Employee Verification Portal.

## 2. Access levels
- **L1 - General:** Lobby, cafeteria, meeting rooms on the ground floor, and the employee's own work floor during their shift. Interns and contractors start at L1.
- **L2 - Standard:** Everything in L1, plus all office work floors, the library and the training rooms. Most full-time employees are L2.
- **L3 - Restricted:** Everything in L2, plus the R&D lab, the network operations room and the records room. Given to engineers, team leads and finance staff who need it for their role.
- **L4 - High security:** Everything in L3, plus the server room (data centre) and the finance vault. Given only to IT infrastructure staff, the Head of Security and the CFO's office. L4 access must be re-approved by the Head of Security every 6 months.

## 3. Zone summary
| Zone | Minimum level |
|---|---|
| Lobby, cafeteria, ground-floor meeting rooms | L1 |
| Office work floors, library, training rooms | L2 |
| R&D lab, network operations room, records room | L3 |
| Server room (data centre), finance vault | L4 |

## 4. Employee status rules
- **Active:** access follows the access level above.
- **On Leave:** no access to L3 or L4 zones. Entry to the office is allowed only with written approval from HR or the reporting manager.
- **Suspended:** all access is blocked. Security must not allow entry and must inform HR immediately.
- **Exited:** the employee is no longer with the company. All access is revoked from the last working day. Treat the person as a visitor.

## 5. After-hours access
Office hours are 08:00 to 20:00, Monday to Saturday. Entry outside these hours, or on Sundays and public holidays, needs prior approval from the reporting manager in the access system. L4 zones may never be entered alone after hours: a second L4 employee or a security officer must be present.

## 6. Escorts
An employee may escort a person with a lower level into a zone only if the escorting employee holds the required level for that zone and stays with them for the whole visit. Visitors must always be escorted beyond the lobby. Nobody may be escorted into the server room except authorised vendor engineers with a valid work order.

## 7. Requesting a higher access level
The employee raises a request in the HR portal with a business reason. The reporting manager approves first, then Corporate Security. Approval for L3 takes up to 3 working days; approval for L4 takes up to 7 working days and needs the Head of Security's sign-off.

## 8. Violations
Entering a zone above one's access level, lending an ID card, or tailgating (following someone through a secured door) is a security violation. A first violation leads to a written warning; repeated or deliberate violations can lead to suspension.
