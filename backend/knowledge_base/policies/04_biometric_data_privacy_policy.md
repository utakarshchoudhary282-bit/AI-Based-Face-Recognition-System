# Biometric Data and Privacy Policy

Company: Orion Nexus Technologies Pvt. Ltd. (fictional) | Policy owner: Data Protection Officer | Version 1.2

## 1. What we collect
For face verification we store: face photos uploaded at enrolment, aligned 112x112 face crops used to train the recognition model, the trained model file, and verification logs (who was checked, by which portal user, when, the similarity score and the result). We do not store photos taken during day-to-day verification; only the log entry is kept.

## 2. Consent
Face photos are collected only after the employee gives written consent at enrolment. An employee may withdraw consent at any time by writing to the Data Protection Officer; they will then be verified with their ID card instead. Withdrawing consent has no effect on their job or evaluation.

## 3. Purpose limitation
Biometric data is used only for identity verification and office access. It must not be used for monitoring productivity, tracking movement inside the office beyond entry points, or any purpose not listed in this policy.

## 4. Who can access the data
- **Admins** (HR and Corporate Security) can enrol employees, upload photos, retrain the model and view verification logs.
- **Security staff** can run verifications and see the result card, but cannot download photos, see logs or change employee data.
- Nobody may copy face photos or the model file outside the portal.

## 5. Retention and deletion
- Verification logs: kept for 90 days, then deleted.
- Face photos and crops of an exited employee: deleted within 30 days of the last working day, after which the model is retrained without them.
- On consent withdrawal: photos deleted and model retrained within 15 days.

## 6. Security of the data
The portal uses individual logins with hashed passwords, role-based access and login tokens that expire after 8 hours. Data is stored on company servers only. Any suspected leak must be reported to the Data Protection Officer within 24 hours.

## 7. Legal basis
This policy is designed to follow applicable data protection law, such as India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023. Where the law and this policy differ, the law applies.
